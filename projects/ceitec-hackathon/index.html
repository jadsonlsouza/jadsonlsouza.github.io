CEITEC Hackathon

Founder and General Coordinator of a student hackathon connecting technology, sustainability, and entrepreneurship.

I am the founder and General Coordinator of the CEITEC Hackathon, an educational event affiliated with the Liga de Inovação e Empreendedorismo da UFRN/IMD (Innovation and Entrepreneurship League at UFRN’s Instituto Metrópole Digital).

The hackathon brings school students together to explore how technology can respond to real-world challenges, with an emphasis on sustainability, creativity, and entrepreneurship. It connects my work in computing education with opportunities for students to develop ideas collaboratively and present their own solutions.

About the event

The CEITEC Hackathon is structured as a two-day experience at CEI Roberto Freire, welcoming students from grades 6–9 and the first two years of upper secondary education. Working in teams of four to six, participants move from identifying a problem to developing a prototype and delivering a final pitch.

The event encourages students to combine Computational Thinking, technical experimentation, and entrepreneurial thinking. Teams consider both how their solution works and why it matters to the people who might use it.

From an idea to a pitch

The first day focuses on immersion and ideation, including guided planning with 5W2H and checkpoints with mentors. The second day combines project development with learning tracks covering AI-assisted prototyping, hardware, design, presentation skills, and startup development.

Mentoring and practice pitches help teams refine their proposals before presenting to a judging panel. Assessment considers teamwork, the relevance and quality of the solution, Computational Thinking and technical feasibility, presentation, and the proposed market or business model.
