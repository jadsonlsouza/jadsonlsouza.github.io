**Status:** Ongoing doctoral research at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN).

My current doctoral research explores the generation of **Flowchart-Based Worked Examples** using generative artificial intelligence. These explanations combine **flowcharts and explanatory text** to represent solutions to introductory programming problems.

The work currently focuses on **generating visual explanations and assessing their quality**. It does not yet report an evaluation of learning outcomes with students.

### Research themes

- Visual explanations for introductory programming
- Generative AI in computing education
- Worked examples and computational thinking

See [publications](/publications/) for related peer-reviewed work.
