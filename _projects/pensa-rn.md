### About the program

Pensa RN aims to foster a culture of educational innovation by integrating **Computational Thinking into the everyday teaching practices of primary and secondary schools**. The program encourages teachers and students to share ideas and approach learning through problem-solving.

Its goals include developing analytical and logical reasoning, critical and creative thinking, and collaboration. These skills connect computing education with broader classroom learning and give educators ways to explore Computational Thinking across their teaching practices.

### My role as a teacher trainer

As a **teacher trainer**, I contribute to the program's teacher professional development work. This is one of the educational initiatives through which I connect Computational Thinking with classroom practice and support teachers in bringing it into their own educational contexts.

### From professional development to classroom practice

Teacher development is a central part of Pensa RN. The program combines theory and practice through playful learning activities, including digital games, to help educators plan their own school-based activities.

Beyond teacher development, the program describes student programming clubs and diagnostic assessment activities that help schools understand students' learning needs. Together, these activities connect educator preparation, student learning, and reflection on teaching practice.
