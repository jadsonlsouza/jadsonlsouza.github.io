I am a **PhD student in Systems and Computing** at the [Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN)](https://www.ufrn.br/), Brazil. My research explores how **generative artificial intelligence** can be used to create **flowchart-based worked examples** and visual explanations to support introductory programming education.

My academic interests connect **Computing Education, Computational Thinking, Generative AI, and Educational Technology**. In my current doctoral work, I investigate the generation and quality of visual explanations that combine flowcharts with explanatory text.

Alongside research, I teach **Computational Thinking** at Colégio CEI, where I have worked since 2020. I also served as a **substitute lecturer at UFRN (2024–2026)**, teaching introductory programming and programming languages. These experiences connect my research questions with the day-to-day practice of computing education.

I hold a **Master's degree in Educational Technology Innovation** (UFRN, 2021) and a **Bachelor's degree in Science and Technology** (UFRN, 2018).

## Research interests

- Generative AI for computing education
- Flowchart-based worked examples and visual explanations
- Introductory programming and computational thinking
- Educational technology and teacher professional development
